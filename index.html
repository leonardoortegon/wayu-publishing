A song has
two halves.

One song, two separate copyrights, and different ways to get paid. Understanding the difference is the first step to knowing what you're owed.

That's us

The composition

Melody + lyrics · Songwriters & publishers

Earns mechanical royalties every time it's streamed or downloaded, performance royalties when it's played in public, and sync fees when it's paired with picture.

The sound recording

The master · Artist or label

The actual recorded version of the song. Earns from streams, downloads, physical sales, neighboring rights and sync of that specific recording.

M

Mechanical

Earn royalties when your music is streamed on demand, downloaded, or reproduced on physical formats like vinyl and CDs.

Performance

Earn royalties when your music is streamed, broadcast, or played in public, from Spotify and radio to live shows, clubs, restaurants, and shops.

Our story

We're music people who believe independent creators deserve a fair shot. We're not here just to manage your music. We want to be part of your team, part of your story, and grow together.

Julian CastañedaCo-founder
Leonardo Ortegon (MIICHII)Co-founder

Two friends, two perspectives. MIICHII, a lifelong musician with a degree in Music Business, knew firsthand how frustrating publishing could be. His longtime friend Julian Castañeda, a developer and music executive with roots in 2Many Records and founder of WAYU Records in 2017, saw an opportunity to bring technology and his industry experience into music publishing.

One shared idea. Between missing royalties, scattered registrations, and labels collecting on behalf of artists without providing statements, it was clear something needed to change. Together, they set out to build a simpler, more transparent way to manage publishing, starting with MIICHII's own catalog.

Built for more than us. What began as a project between friends quickly proved its value. Realizing other independent artists faced the same challenges, they turned their solution into WAYU Publishing. Not just to manage music, but to become part of the team, part of the story, and grow together.
